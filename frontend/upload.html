Upload PDF documents to process and search within your personal collection

Upload lets you add your own PDF documents to be processed and included in searches.

  1. Drag and drop a PDF file (or click to browse).
  2. The system will extract text, generate embeddings, and make the document searchable across Patient Matching, Evaluate Treatment Options, and other modes.
  3. Log in to keep your uploads permanently; otherwise they are session-only.
