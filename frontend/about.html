About Paxis

Paxis is an AI-powered medical literature search platform designed to assist oncologists in finding evidence-based information, matching patients to relevant studies, and comparing treatment options.

What is Paxis?

Paxis is a comprehensive AI-powered platform that transforms how oncologists and medical professionals access, analyze, and utilize medical literature. Built specifically for oncology, the platform provides instant access to evidence-based information from thousands of peer-reviewed research papers, clinical trials, and medical studies.

The platform uses advanced artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and semantic search technologies to understand complex medical queries and deliver precise, cited answers. Instead of spending hours searching through databases, oncologists can ask questions in natural language and receive comprehensive, evidence-based responses with proper source attribution.

Paxis goes beyond simple search by offering specialized tools for patient matching and treatment comparison, enabling clinicians to make more informed decisions based on the latest research evidence.

How It Works

1. Document Processing

Medical literature is processed using OCR and vision models to extract text, tables, and figures. Structured study profiles are automatically extracted.

2. Semantic Indexing

Content is converted to vector embeddings and stored for semantic search, enabling understanding beyond simple keyword matching.

3. Intelligent Retrieval

Queries are analyzed for intent and matched against the knowledge base using hybrid search with relevance reranking.

4. AI Synthesis

Retrieved evidence is synthesized into comprehensive answers with proper citations and source attribution.

Our Mission

Paxis aims to revolutionize how oncologists access and utilize medical literature. By leveraging advanced AI and natural language processing, we provide instant access to evidence-based information from thousands of research papers, enabling faster and more informed clinical decision-making.

Our platform combines semantic search, patient matching algorithms, and treatment comparison tools to deliver comprehensive, accurate, and actionable insights from the latest oncology research.
