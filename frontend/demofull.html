Implicit → explicit inference

Reads clinical language the way literature phrases it

The chart says one thing, the literature says another. Paxis's ontology-inference layer bridges them automatically:

"no longer a surgical candidate"→unresectableinoperable
"progressing on pembrolizumab"→ICI-refractoryanti-PD1 failure
Multi-axis parallel search

Searches 8 clinical axes at once, not one query

Cancer site, histology, biomarkers, treatment trajectory, comorbidities, and staging are each embedded and searched separately across Qdrant, PostgreSQL structured records, and patient-outcome frames in parallel — then merged. A single search box can't hold that.
