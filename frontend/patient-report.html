Explain my report

Paste a pathology, imaging, or lab report and get the terminology explained in everyday words.

What this does and doesn't do. It explains what the words and abbreviations mean. It will not tell you whether a result is good or bad, what it means for your diagnosis or outlook, or what happens next. The same words can mean very different things depending on your full picture, and only your care team can put them in context.
