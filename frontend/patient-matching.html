Patient Matching

Find clinical studies that match your patient. Use free text or the structured form.

Patient Matching searches our library of curated oncology studies to find ones that best fit your patient.

  1. Choose Free text to describe your patient naturally, or Structured form to enter details field-by-field.
  2. The system extracts clinical entities (cancer type, stage, biomarkers, etc.) and scores every study for relevance.
  3. Results show a match score, key study details, and a link to the full study profile.

Include whatever you have: age, sex, cancer type, stage, histology, biomarkers (e.g. EGFR+, HER2-), performance status (ECOG), smoking status, comorbidities.
