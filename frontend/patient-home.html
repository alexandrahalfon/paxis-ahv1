How it works

Three steps, and you can stop after the first.

1

Ask in your own words

No medical terms needed. If you're not sure of the details, that's completely normal.

2

Get a clear answer

Explained simply, with the research it came from, so you can see where it's based.

3

Connect your doctor, optionally

If they give you a code, answers use your actual diagnosis and you can send questions to them.

What Paxis will and won't do.
It explains things, helps you prepare questions, and points you to the research behind an answer. It will not diagnose you, tell you what your results mean for your outlook, or suggest changing anything about your treatment. Those belong to your care team, who know your full picture.

If something feels urgent, contact your care team or your local emergency number right away rather than asking here.
