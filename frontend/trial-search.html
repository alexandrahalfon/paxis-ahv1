Trial Finder

Search ClinicalTrials.gov using a patient case or structured details.

Trial Finder searches ClinicalTrials.gov for clinical trials that may be relevant to your patient.

  1. Describe your patient case in the text box — include cancer type, stage, biomarkers, age, ECOG, and any prior treatments.
  2. The system extracts structured details automatically using AI.
  3. Optionally expand Advanced fields & filters to override or add details (e.g., location, recruiting status, trial phase).
  4. Results are ranked by how well each trial matches your patient profile.

Include age, sex, cancer type/site, stage, histology, biomarkers, ECOG if known.
