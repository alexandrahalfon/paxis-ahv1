Evaluate Treatment Options

Describe what you want to compare and get visual analysis with supporting evidence

Evaluate Treatment Options lets you compare two or more treatments using evidence from our study library.

  1. Type a natural-language question — e.g., "Compare pembrolizumab vs chemotherapy for stage IV NSCLC".
  2. The system identifies the treatments and cancer context, retrieves matching studies, and generates a visual side-by-side comparison.
  3. Results include survival outcomes, toxicity profiles, and supporting evidence citations.
